# Hebbia

**A document-scale research agent for finance and diligence, not a generic chatbot.**

## What it actually does
Hebbia’s Matrix product runs structured analysis across large sets of filings, contracts, and internal documents. Matrix 2.0 can take a request, build the working table, and push toward a memo, deck, or model. Max is the newer dealmaking-oriented agent layer.

## Key Features
- Multi-document analysis at deal scale
- Structured tables across hundreds of sources
- Path from research to memo / deck / model
- Built for finance and legal diligence

## Pricing
Enterprise only. No public self-serve plan. Third-party reports put seats in the thousands of dollars per year.

## Strengths
- Designed for the actual workflow of investors and bankers
- Stronger structure than plain “summarize this PDF”
- Can finish more of the deliverable, not just extract quotes

## Limitations
- Closed and expensive
- Not a general computer-use agent
- Competitive market is getting crowded

## Best for
Investment, diligence, and document-heavy finance teams.

## Skip if
You want a $20/month personal agent.

## Final Verdict
A serious vertical research agent if your work is deal documents. **Score: 8.2/10**
