# Lindy

**An AI assistant focused on handling real productivity work with meaningful autonomy.**

## What it actually does
Lindy can manage email, schedule meetings, handle tasks, and run multi-step workflows across common productivity tools. It is designed to act more like a capable executive assistant than a simple chatbot.

## Key Features
- Email and calendar management
- Multi-step workflow execution
- Integration with common productivity tools
- Memory and context across tasks

## Pricing
Paid subscription plans (exact pricing varies by tier).

## Strengths
- Strong focus on real productivity outcomes
- Good at email and scheduling workflows
- Feels more assistant-like than most tools

## Limitations
- Less strong at deep coding or complex computer use
- Pricing can add up for heavy use

## Best for
Founders, executives, and professionals who want to offload email, scheduling, and routine coordination.

## Final Verdict
One of the better productivity-focused autonomous assistants currently available. **Score: 8.1/10**
