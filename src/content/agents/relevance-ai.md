# Relevance AI

**A platform for building teams of AI agents that work together on real business tasks.**

## What it actually does
Relevance AI lets you create and orchestrate multiple specialized agents that can research, analyze, write, and take actions. It is commonly used for sales research, market analysis, content, and operational workflows.

## Key Features
- Multi-agent orchestration
- Research and analysis tools
- Custom agent building
- Integrations with business tools

## Pricing
Usage-based pricing with paid plans.

## Strengths
- Strong multi-agent capabilities
- Good for business research and sales workflows
- Flexible agent design

## Limitations
- Requires more setup than simpler assistants
- Can become complex as agent teams grow

## Best for
Teams that want to build custom multi-agent systems for research, sales, or operations.

## Final Verdict
A solid platform for people who want more control over multi-agent workflows. **Score: 8.0/10**
