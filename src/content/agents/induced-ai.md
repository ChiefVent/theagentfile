# Induced AI

**A browser agent aimed at enterprise processes, not consumer shopping tasks.**

## What it actually does
Induced builds autonomous browser agents for business workflows that still live in web apps with weak or missing APIs: procurement, vendor portals, compliance checks, and repetitive back-office clicks.

## Key Features
- Autonomous browser for enterprise apps
- Workflow automation across web UIs
- Aimed at ops and back-office teams
- Better fit for internal process work than consumer browsing

## Pricing
Enterprise / sales-led.

## Strengths
- Focused on ugly real business websites
- More practical than demo-only web agents
- Good category fit for “no API, still have to click”

## Limitations
- Not a household-name consumer product
- Needs process design and monitoring
- Browser automation still breaks when UIs change

## Best for
Ops teams drowning in portal work that will never get a proper API.

## Skip if
You want a personal research assistant.

## Final Verdict
A narrower but useful browser-agent product for enterprise busywork. **Score: 7.6/10**
