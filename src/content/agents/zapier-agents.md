# Zapier Agents

**Agents that sit on top of Zapier’s app network and can actually move work between tools.**

## What it actually does
Zapier Agents use natural language plus Zapier’s integrations to research, decide, and take actions across connected apps. They are most useful when the work already lives in SaaS tools Zapier supports.

## Key Features
- Huge integration catalog
- Natural-language agent setup
- Actions across email, CRM, sheets, Slack, and more
- Good fit for operators, not just engineers

## Pricing
Free tier with limited activity. Paid plans commonly start around $33/month.

## Strengths
- Fastest path if your stack is already in Zapier
- Practical business actions, not just chat
- Lower technical barrier than frameworks

## Limitations
- Less autonomous than a full computer-use agent
- Complex flows can get expensive
- Quality depends on how well the apps are connected

## Best for
Ops and marketing teams who want agents that can use existing SaaS tools.

## Skip if
You need desktop computer use or deep coding autonomy.

## Final Verdict
Not the most autonomous agent, but one of the most useful if your work already runs through Zapier. **Score: 7.8/10**
