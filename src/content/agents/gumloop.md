# Gumloop

**A builder for team-owned agents, not a single boxed assistant.**

## What it actually does
Gumloop lets non-technical teams create agents and workflows that watch apps, process data, and take action. It is commonly used for GTM, research, enrichment, and repeating marketing or ops jobs.

## Key Features
- No-code agent builder
- App triggers and long-running tasks
- Skills that can be reused across the team
- Team-wide agent libraries

## Pricing
Pro commonly starts around $37/month with included credits. Heavier use adds usage cost.

## Strengths
- Fast for operators, not just engineers
- Good for GTM and marketing workflows
- Agents can be shared across a team

## Limitations
- Not full desktop computer use
- Usage can climb on large jobs
- Quality depends on how well the workflow is designed

## Best for
Marketing, sales, and ops teams that want to build their own agents.

## Skip if
You need one general autonomous worker with its own computer.

## Final Verdict
One of the better no-code agent builders for GTM and operations. **Score: 8.0/10**
