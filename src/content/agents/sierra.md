# Sierra

**Enterprise support agents built to resolve customer issues, not just deflect them.**

## What it actually does
Sierra builds branded customer-experience agents for large companies. They can work across chat, voice, and email, follow company policy, and complete support workflows with guardrails.

## Key Features
- Cross-channel support agents
- Voice and chat as first-class surfaces
- Brand and policy controls
- Enterprise rollout and governance

## Pricing
Enterprise-only. Public pricing is not listed. Reported contracts are large.

## Strengths
- Strong enterprise CX focus
- Voice plus digital channels
- Built for brand-sensitive companies

## Limitations
- Not self-serve
- Expensive
- Overkill below serious support volume

## Best for
Large companies that want a branded support agent with real resolution capacity.

## Skip if
You are a small team looking for a $20/month tool.

## Final Verdict
A serious enterprise support agent, not a directory toy. **Score: 8.1/10**
