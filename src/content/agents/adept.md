# Adept

**An AI lab focused on agents that can use software tools to complete work.**

## What it actually does
Adept builds models and systems designed to take actions inside software tools rather than just generating text. Their focus is on creating agents that can operate across real applications.

## Key Features
- Action-taking agent models
- Tool use across software
- Research-focused development

## Pricing
Primarily enterprise / limited access.

## Strengths
- Strong research focus on action-taking agents
- Ambitious long-term vision

## Limitations
- Limited public product access
- Not as widely available as other platforms

## Best for
Teams interested in cutting-edge agent research and enterprise partnerships.

## Final Verdict
Interesting research-driven player in the agent space, though less accessible than others. **Score: 7.8/10**
