# Paradox

**A recruiting agent built for high-volume hiring, not one-off executive search.**

## What it actually does
Paradox, now part of Workday, runs candidate-experience agents that talk to applicants, answer questions, schedule interviews, send reminders, and keep frontline hiring moving. It is widely used by large employers with constant hourly and store-level hiring.

## Key Features
- 24/7 candidate Q&A
- Automated self-scheduling
- Reminders and rescheduling
- ATS / Workday integration

## Pricing
Enterprise. Sold through Workday / talent-acquisition deals, not a cheap self-serve plan.

## Strengths
- Excellent at high-volume frontline hiring
- Real actions, not just resume chat
- Strong brand-name customer base

## Limitations
- Not useful for a solo founder hiring one engineer
- Enterprise sales cycle
- Narrow outside recruiting

## Best for
Companies hiring a lot of people, especially hourly and frontline roles.

## Skip if
You want a general agent or a tool for personal productivity.

## Final Verdict
One of the few recruiting agents that clearly does production work at scale. **Score: 8.0/10**
