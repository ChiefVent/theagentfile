# Perplexity Comet

**A research-first agentic browser rather than a general employee platform.**

## What it actually does
Comet is Perplexity’s agentic browser layer. It is built for finding information, citing sources, and taking actions in the browser when a web task needs more than a search result.

## Key Features
- Agentic browser workflow
- Cited research
- Good for source-backed answers
- Lower friction than many standalone agent products

## Pricing
Perplexity has a free tier. Pro is commonly around $20/month. Comet access has been broader than most paid-only agent products.

## Strengths
- Excellent for research with sources
- Easy on-ramp
- Stronger than a normal search box for multi-step web questions

## Limitations
- Narrower than full computer-use platforms
- Not built as a persistent multi-bot workforce
- Less useful for deep coding or CRM operations

## Best for
People who want an agent for research and web tasks, not a whole digital staff.

## Skip if
You need scheduled multi-tool business workflows.

## Final Verdict
One of the better research-and-browser agents, especially if sources matter. **Score: 8.0/10**
