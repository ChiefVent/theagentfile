# Harvey

**Legal agents built around matters, files, permissions, and how a firm actually works.**

## What it actually does
Harvey is an enterprise legal platform. Its agents can work inside a matter or project, start with the right files and permissions, and take on multi-step legal work like contract review, diligence, and research. Harvey II added stronger memory and a legal-specific model.

## Key Features
- Matter- and project-based agents
- Document-heavy legal workflows
- Memory of lawyer preferences
- Enterprise permissions and review loops

## Pricing
Enterprise only. No meaningful self-serve plan.

## Strengths
- Built for real legal work, not generic chat
- Strong adoption in large firms
- Agents start with matter context instead of a blank prompt

## Limitations
- Not available to most individuals
- Expensive and sales-led
- Narrow outside legal work

## Best for
Law firms and in-house legal teams doing document-heavy work.

## Skip if
You want a consumer or general-purpose agent.

## Final Verdict
One of the strongest vertical agents in production, if you can get access. **Score: 8.3/10**
