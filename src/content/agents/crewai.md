# CrewAI

**A popular framework for building teams of AI agents that collaborate.**

## What it actually does
CrewAI lets you define multiple specialized agents with different roles and have them work together on complex tasks. It is widely used for research, content, analysis, and multi-step business workflows.

## Key Features
- Multi-agent crews with defined roles
- Task delegation and collaboration
- Open-source foundation with hosted options
- Flexible tool integration

## Pricing
Open-source core. Paid hosted and enterprise options available.

## Strengths
- Excellent for multi-agent collaboration
- Strong developer community
- Flexible and composable

## Limitations
- Requires more technical setup than fully managed products
- Quality depends heavily on how well agents are designed

## Best for
Developers and technical teams who want to build custom multi-agent systems.

## Final Verdict
One of the most popular and practical frameworks for multi-agent workflows. **Score: 8.0/10**
