# Eightfold

**A recruiting system with agents on top of talent intelligence, not just a chatbot bolted onto an ATS.**

## What it actually does
Eightfold is a talent-intelligence platform. Its newer agents can talk to candidates, help with screening and matching, and sit on top of a skills graph rather than keyword filters alone.

## Key Features
- Skills-based matching
- Candidate conversation agents
- Talent intelligence across hiring and internal mobility
- Enterprise HR stack

## Pricing
Enterprise.

## Strengths
- Stronger talent graph than most chat recruiters
- Useful beyond a single job req
- Built for large employers

## Limitations
- Heavy platform, not a lightweight agent
- Sales-led and expensive
- Not a general-purpose worker

## Best for
Large companies that already think in talent intelligence, not just job slots.

## Skip if
You are hiring one person this quarter and need a simple tool.

## Final Verdict
A real recruiting-agent layer, but it is a platform first. **Score: 7.7/10**
